Delete your account

Request deletion of your The Match Report account and associated personal data.

Request deletion without the app

Email admin@thematchreportapp.com from the email address connected with your The Match Report account. Use the subject “The Match Report account deletion request”.

Email account deletion request

Include the name shown on your account. Do not send your password. We may ask you to verify that you control the account before completing the request.

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to The Match Report.
  2. Open the app menu.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Read the deletion information and confirm Delete account.

If the app asks you to sign in again, sign out, sign back in and repeat the deletion steps. This security check confirms that the request is being made by the account holder.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in identity and user profile.
  • Your memberships of teams connected with the account.
  • Your private messages and registered device records.
  • Other private app data associated only with your account.

What may be retained

Deleting an account does not automatically delete sponsored teams or their shared content. Shared team records, such as match reports and attendance history, may remain where other authorised team members need them.

Transaction, security, fraud-prevention, tax, dispute-resolution and other legally required records may also be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes. Deleted information may remain temporarily in secure backups until those backups are overwritten through the service provider’s normal backup cycle.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel the subscription separately through the store where it was purchased if you do not want it to renew.
