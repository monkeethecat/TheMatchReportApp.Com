Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

The Match Report (“we”, “us”) provides an app for grassroots and junior football clubs to manage squads, record matches and share match reports. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and sign-in method (email/password or Google) so you can sign in on iPhone and Android.
  • Club and team information entered by club staff: club name, crest, sponsor details, fixtures, training sessions and venues.
  • Player profiles: name, nickname, squad number, position, optional photo or illustration, optional date of birth and profile notes.
  • Match data: line-ups, match events (goals, assists, passes, shots, cards, substitutions), minutes played, statistics, manager and coach comments and Player of the Match awards.
  • Attendance and votes: attendance replies and community Player of the Match votes. Votes are kept confidential.
  • Messages and notifications: team announcements, plus a device token so we can send push notifications.
  • Voice events: if you use the microphone during a live game, your speech is turned into text using your device’s speech recognition service. Only the text you choose to save is stored.

How we use it

  • To run the app’s features: squads, live match recording, reports, statistics, attendance and messaging.
  • To let approved members of your team see the team’s information.
  • To send the notifications you’ve enabled, such as match events, attendance requests and voting alerts.
  • To keep the service secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Children’s information

Junior teams may include information about players under 18. This information is entered and managed by club staff and linked parents or guardians. Parent links to a child player must be approved by a team owner, manager or admin. Clubs are responsible for making sure they have the right consent to add players’ details.

Who can see your information

Team information is visible only to approved members of that team, according to their role (owner, admin, manager, coach, player, parent or supporter). Match reports you choose to share, for example to WhatsApp, are shared by you through that service.

Where it’s stored

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Messaging). Google processes this data on our behalf.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep information while your account or team is active. Club staff can delete players and match reports in the app. To delete your account and associated data, contact us at the address below.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how it’s used. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
