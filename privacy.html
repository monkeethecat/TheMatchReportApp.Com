2 Information we collect and process

We collect personal information that is necessary to create and operate your account and to provide the service, including account details, team information, player information, match data, messages, device information, subscription information, and any optional profile content you choose to provide.

3 How we use information

We use your information to create and maintain user accounts, authenticate users, operate team and club features, generate reports, send notifications, provide customer support, process subscriptions, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

4 Our lawful bases

We process account, team and subscription information where necessary to provide the service, fulfil our contract with you, pursue our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app, and where consent is required or appropriate.

5 Children’s information

The Match Report is designed primarily for adult club owners, managers, coaches, administrators, parents and fans. Junior teams may contain information about players under 18, entered and managed by authorised club staff or linked parents and guardians.

6 Who can see team information

Team information is available only to approved members according to their assigned role and permissions. Owners, managers, coaches and appointed administrators may edit authorised team information. Players, parents, fans and other read-only members may view information made available to them.

7 Sharing reports outside the app

The Match Report may allow you to share a report or other content through another application, such as WhatsApp, email or messaging services. Once information is sent to another service, that service processes it under its own privacy policy and terms.

8 Service providers and recipients

We use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Installations for account authentication, database storage, push notifications, application installation identifiers and operational security. Google processes information on our behalf and may also process certain information under its own terms. We also use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and Apple/Google Play for app-store purchases.

9 International processing

Some service providers, including Google, Apple and RevenueCat, may process information in countries outside the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we rely on appropriate legal protections as required by applicable law.

10 Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including encrypted network connections, Firebase authentication, role-based access controls, restricted team membership, server-controlled subscription records and limited administrative access.

11 How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including account management, team operations, subscription records, support communications and legal requirements.

12 Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal information by emailing admin@thematchreportapp.com from the email address associated with your account. We may need to verify your identity before completing a deletion request.

When an account is deleted, we will delete or anonymise personal information associated with that account unless retention is required by law, needed to prevent fraud or resolve a dispute, or part of a shared team record that other authorised users must retain.

Deleting an account is different from leaving a team. Leaving a team removes or changes team access but does not necessarily delete the account.

13 Your data-protection rights

Depending on your circumstances and the lawful basis used, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict processing, object to processing, receive certain information in a portable format, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

14 Complaints

Please contact us first if you have a privacy concern so that we can try to resolve it. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

15 Automated decisions

We use automated checks to determine whether a subscription is active and which paid features should be available. These checks control app access but are not used to make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about individuals.

16 Required and optional information

Account information such as an email address and authentication identifier is required to create and operate an account. Other information, such as player photos, dates of birth, profile notes, notification permissions and microphone access, may be optional depending on how the club uses the app.

17 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, service providers, legal requirements or data practices change. We will publish the updated policy at the same web address and change the Last updated date.
