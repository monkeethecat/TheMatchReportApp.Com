The Match Report is operated by FatPig (The Match Report, we, us or our). The Match Report provides mobile applications for grassroots and junior football clubs to manage teams, squads, fixtures, training, attendance, live matches, statistics, communications and match reports.

This privacy policy explains what personal information The Match Report collects or receives, why we use it, who may see it, how long it is retained and the choices available to you. This policy applies to The Match Report applications on Android and iOS.

1 Who is responsible for your information

For information connected with your account, subscriptions and operation of The Match Report service, the data controller is:

FatPig

Email admin@thematchreportapp.com

Clubs, teams and their authorised staff may also have responsibilities under data-protection law for information they enter about players, parents, staff and other team members. Clubs are responsible for ensuring they have an appropriate lawful basis and any necessary permissions to enter that information.

2 Information we collect and process

The information processed depends on the features you use and the role you hold.

Account and authentication information

  • Your name and display name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your unique account identifier.
  • Your selected account role.
  • Your sign-in provider, such as email and password or Google Sign-In.
  • Authentication and account-security information.
  • Club and team memberships linked to your account.

Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. We do not receive or store your password in readable form.

Club and team information

  • Club and team names.
  • Club crests and team images.
  • Sponsor names, images and information.
  • Seasons and competitions.
  • Fixtures, results, venues and training sessions.
  • Team announcements and administrative information.
  • Staff appointments and membership roles.

Player information

  • Player name and nickname.
  • Squad number and playing position.
  • Optional photograph, illustration or profile image.
  • Optional date of birth.
  • Profile notes and biography.
  • Team membership.
  • Match availability and attendance.
  • Playing history, statistics and awards.
  • Parent or guardian links.

Providing optional information, such as a photograph or date of birth, is not required unless the club decides it is necessary for team administration.

Match and performance information

  • Team selections and line-ups.
  • Match events, including goals, assists, shots, passes, saves, cards and substitutions.
  • Playing time and positions.
  • Match results and statistics.
  • Manager and Coach comments.
  • Player ratings and Player of the Match awards.
  • Live-match event information.
  • Match reports and shared report content.

Attendance and voting

  • Fixture and training attendance responses.
  • Availability information.
  • Community Player of the Match votes.

Individual votes are not intended to be displayed publicly to other users. Aggregated results may be displayed to authorised team members.

Messages and notifications

  • Team announcements and messages.
  • Information about the sender, recipients and team.
  • Message timestamps.
  • Notification preferences.
  • A Firebase device or application token used to deliver push notifications.

Notifications may include fixture reminders, attendance requests, match updates, team announcements and voting alerts.

Microphone and voice recognition

The Android app may request microphone permission when you deliberately use voice entry during a live match. Your device's speech-recognition service converts speech into text. The Match Report stores only the text that you choose to save as a match event. We do not intentionally store an audio recording of your voice.

Your device platform or speech-recognition provider may process audio according to its own settings and privacy terms. You can use the app without enabling microphone permission by entering match events manually.

Subscription and purchase information

Club Owners and independent Managers may purchase subscriptions through Apple's App Store or Google Play.

  • The subscription product selected.
  • Purchase and transaction identifiers.
  • Subscription status.
  • Start, renewal and expiry dates.
  • Cancellation, refund and billing-status information.
  • The RevenueCat customer identifier associated with your account.
  • Subscription entitlements, such as Club Owner or Team Manager access.
  • Store and platform information.

Payments are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number, bank-account number or card security code.

RevenueCat helps us validate purchases and determine whether subscription access is active.

Appointed Managers, Coaches and Administrators do not need to purchase their own subscription when their access is sponsored by a Club Owner.

Device and technical information

  • Device or application identifiers.
  • Operating system and app version.
  • Firebase installation identifier.
  • Push-notification token.
  • Network and request information.
  • Diagnostic, security and purchase-validation information.
  • Dates and times that the app communicates with supporting services.

We do not use this information for third-party advertising.

Information entered about another person

Club staff, team staff and linked parents may enter information about players or other team members.

If you enter information about another person, you must have permission or another lawful basis to do so and must ensure that the information is accurate and appropriate.

3 How we use information

  • Create and maintain user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Operate team, squad and club-management features.
  • Manage team roles and permissions.
  • Record fixtures, attendance, match events and statistics.
  • Generate and display match reports.
  • Provide messaging and team announcements.
  • Send notifications requested or enabled by users.
  • Link parents or guardians with player profiles following approval.
  • Process, validate and restore subscriptions.
  • Determine whether a team has active sponsored access.
  • Make a team read-only if its sponsoring subscription expires.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Investigate technical, security or misuse issues.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations.
  • Establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for third-party advertising or behavioural advertising.

4 Our lawful bases

Contract

We process account, team and subscription information where necessary to provide the service you request and perform our agreement with you.

Legitimate interests

We process information where necessary for legitimate interests including operating and improving the service, supporting clubs and teams, preventing fraud and misuse, protecting accounts and data, providing customer support, maintaining accurate subscription access and diagnosing technical problems. We consider whether these interests are outweighed by the rights and interests of affected individuals.

Consent

We rely on consent where appropriate, including when you choose to enable microphone access, enable push notifications, or add optional profile information or images where consent is the appropriate basis. You may withdraw device permissions through your device settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that lawfully occurred before withdrawal.

Legal obligation

We may process and retain information where necessary to comply with legal, tax, accounting, court or regulatory requirements.

Clubs and team staff are responsible for determining the appropriate lawful basis for player and junior-team information they enter.

5 Children's information

The Match Report is designed primarily for adult Club Owners, Managers, Coaches, Administrators, Parents and Fans.

Junior teams may contain information about players under 18. This information is entered and managed by authorised club staff or linked parents and guardians.

Parent or guardian links to a player must be approved by authorised team staff.

Clubs and authorised users are responsible for:

  • Having an appropriate lawful basis to add a child's information.
  • Obtaining parental or guardian consent where required.
  • Entering only information necessary for team administration.
  • Keeping information accurate.
  • Limiting photographs, dates of birth and profile notes appropriately.
  • Responding to requests from parents, guardians and players.

Children should not create or operate an account unless this is permitted by the club, their parent or guardian and applicable law.

If you believe information about a child has been entered improperly, contact admin@thematchreportapp.com

6 Who can see team information

Team information is available only to approved members according to their assigned role and permissions.

Owners, Managers, Coaches and appointed Administrators may edit authorised team information.

Players, Parents, Fans and other read-only members may view information made available to them.

Linked parents or guardians may view information associated with their linked player.

Subscription status may determine whether a team is editable or read-only.

An expired sponsoring subscription makes the relevant team read-only. Existing team information is not automatically deleted merely because a subscription expires.

Users must not share account credentials or use information obtained through the app for unrelated or unlawful purposes.

7 Sharing reports outside the app

The Match Report may allow you to share a report or other content through another application, such as WhatsApp, email or messaging services.

Sharing occurs only when you choose to use the relevant sharing feature. Once information is sent to another service, that service processes it under its own privacy policy and terms.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have permission to share information about players and team members outside the app.

We may disclose information to professional advisers, courts, law-enforcement bodies, regulators or other authorities when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, users or the service.

We do not allow service providers to use information for their own advertising on our behalf.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we rely on appropriate legal protections as required by applicable law. These may include adequacy regulations, approved contractual safeguards or other recognised transfer mechanisms.

10 Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including:

  • Encrypted network connections.
  • Firebase authentication.
  • Role-based access controls.
  • Firestore security rules.
  • Restricted team membership.
  • Server-controlled subscription records.
  • Limited administrative access.
  • Device and account authentication.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and device access.

If you believe your account or information has been compromised, contact us promptly.

11 How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy.

  • Account information is retained while the account remains active.
  • Team and match information is retained while the team remains active or while authorised members continue to require access.
  • Device notification tokens are retained until they are replaced, invalidated or no longer needed.
  • Subscription records may be retained while the subscription or related account is active and afterwards where necessary for payment, tax, fraud-prevention, dispute or legal purposes.
  • Support correspondence may be retained while the issue is active and for a reasonable period afterwards.
  • Security records may be retained where necessary to investigate misuse or protect the service.
  • Deleted information may remain temporarily in secure backups until those backups are overwritten under the service provider's normal backup cycle.

Where we do not specify a fixed period, we consider:

  • Why the information was collected.
  • Whether it is still required to provide the service.
  • Whether other authorised team members still require shared team records.
  • Legal, accounting and dispute-resolution requirements.
  • The sensitivity and risk associated with continued retention.

We may retain information in anonymised form where it can no longer reasonably identify an individual.

12 Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal information:

  • Inside the app through Account or Settings, then Delete account.
  • Request account deletion by emailing admin@thematchreportapp.com from the email address associated with your account.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a deletion request.

When an account is deleted, we will delete or anonymise personal information associated with that account unless:

  • Retention is required by law.
  • Information is needed to prevent fraud or resolve a dispute.
  • The information forms part of a shared team record that other authorised users must retain.
  • Another lawful reason requires limited retention.

Where information must remain in a shared team record, we will remove or anonymise the deleted user's personal identifiers where reasonably possible.

Deleting an account is different from leaving a team. Leaving a team removes or changes team access but does not necessarily delete the account.

Subscriptions and deletion

Deleting The Match Report account does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription.

You should cancel the subscription through the store where it was purchased:

  • Android: Google Play, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  • iPhone or iPad: Apple Settings, then Apple Account, then Subscriptions.

Similarly, cancelling a subscription does not automatically delete your account or team data. When a sponsoring subscription expires, the affected team normally becomes read-only.
