How it works

Match day in four panels

  1. 1
    Pick!

    Pick your team

    See who’s available, then drag players into your formation for 5, 7, 9 or 11-a-side.

  2. 2
    Peep!

    Kick off

    Start the clock and the live pitch takes over. Your whole squad, in formation, on one screen.

  3. 3
    Thwack!

    Record every moment

    Swipe a pass, flick a shot, drag on a sub or just speak. Every goal, save, card and corner goes on the timeline.

  4. 4
    Ping!

    Publish & share

    Add the manager’s notes, open the Player of the Match vote, then post the full report to WhatsApp.

The star of the show

The Live Game pitch

No clipboards, no soggy notebooks. Your team sits on a live pitch in formation, and every touch becomes part of the match report.

  • Swipe player to player to log passing moves, with the trail drawn right on the pitch.
  • Drag towards goal to take a shot: goal, saved, missed, hit the post or off the bar.
  • One-tap events: corners, free kicks, penalties, yellow and red cards, injuries.
  • Drag a sub from the bench onto the player coming off. Minutes played are tracked for you.
  • Voice events. Tap the mic, say what happened and check the transcript before you save it.
  • GOAL! A full-screen celebration with confetti and haptics, then pick the scorer and the assist.
  • Simple mode for a quick score-and-timeline game, or the full pitch for detailed player stats.

Everything your club needs

Pick Team

Team selection that knows who’s turning up

Choose 5, 7, 9 or 11-a-side and a formation to suit, then fill each position. Attendance replies sit right next to every name, so you can see who’s attending, not attending or still unconfirmed before you pick.

Players

Your squad, drawn like comic heroes

Give every player a number, a nickname and a position from goalkeeper and sweeper-keeper to regista, poacher and false nine. Pick one of the retro comic illustrations or upload a photo. Each profile updates itself with goals, assists and appearances whenever a report is published.

Results

A proper match report, every single week

Every game is saved to your match archive with the score, scorers, assists, line-ups, manager’s and coach’s comments, full match stats and a minute-by-minute commentary. Tap Post to WhatsApp and it’s in the group chat, nicely formatted.

Player of the Match

Let the touchline have its say

Three awards every game: Manager’s, Coach’s and a Community vote for parents, members and supporters. Votes are confidential and can’t be changed, and the report publishes when voting closes.

Built for junior football

Every child’s moment, in print

For under-7s to under-18s, the small moments are the big ones: a first goal, a worldie of a save, a first Player of the Match. The Match Report captures them all and gets them to the family.

👪 Parent links

Parents link to their own child players and can reply to attendance for them. An owner, manager or admin approves every link first.

🔐 Approvals & team codes

People join with a private team code and wait for approval. Admins can approve, reject, suspend or restore access at any time.

🎂 Birthday reminders

Add a date of birth and teammates get a reminder a week before, so nobody’s big day gets missed.

🗳 Fair, confidential votes

Community Player of the Match votes are private and locked in, so it stays about the football.

Support

Questions from the dugout

Does it work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. The Match Report is on the App Store and Google Play, and your account works on both as long as you sign in the same way (Google or email). A manager on iPhone and a parent on Android see the same club.

What match formats are supported?

5, 7, 9 and 11-a-side, each with its own set of formations, so it fits mini-soccer right up to adult 11-a-side.

How do people join my team?

Share your team code from the Club tab, straight to WhatsApp or any other app. New members choose their role and wait for an owner, manager or admin to approve them.

Who can record the live match?

Only owners, managers, coaches and admins can set up and run a live game. Everyone else can follow along, read the reports and vote.

How does Player of the Match work?

The manager and coach each pick a Player of the Match, and supporters, parents and members get a confidential community vote. Votes can’t be changed once cast.

Can I share match reports to WhatsApp?

Yes. Every published report has a “Post to WhatsApp” button that sends a formatted summary with the score, scorers, Player of the Match awards, stats and commentary.

Can I run more than one team?

Yes. Create or join extra teams and switch between them. Each team keeps its own players, fixtures, reports, stats and members.

I need help. How do I get in touch?

Email us at support@thematchreport.app and we’ll get back to you as soon as we can.
